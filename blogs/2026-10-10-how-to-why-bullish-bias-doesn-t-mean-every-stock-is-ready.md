A bullish market bias does not mean every stock offers the same trading conditions. This historical 15-symbol TCP Scanner example illustrates several different market conditions: **HOLD LONG / HOLD SHORT:** The indicator identifies established bullish or bearish continuation conditions—not automatic entry signals. **WAIT:** Conditions do not currently support a directional setup.
