ASML’s Q3 revenue jumped 55% year over year to a record €11.7 billion, slightly below expectations by €70 million. GAAP EPS came in at €12.93, beating estimates by €2.14. Part of that earnings beat came from a €500 million valuation gain on its investment in Mistral AI, but the underlying performance was still strong.
