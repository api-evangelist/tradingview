Fundamental Analysis Gold finished the week strongly, with spot prices reaching around 4,194 on Friday and gaining roughly 1.5% on the session. The rebound was supported by a softer dollar and easing Treasury yields after Gold had fallen to a two-month low earlier in the week. Markets still expect the Fed to remain restrictive, with pricing showing around a 19% chance of an October hike and about 84% probability of at least one hike by December.
